  1. គណនាលីមីតខាងក្រោម៖

    2. $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to +\infty}\dfrac{(2x+1)(3x-2)}{3x^2+2x-5}$
    3. $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to +\infty}\dfrac{\sqrt{3x^2+2}}{4x+1}$
    4. $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to +\infty}\dfrac{\sqrt{3x-2}}{\sqrt{2x^2+1}}$
    5. $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to -\infty}\dfrac{\sqrt{3x^2+3}}{x+3}$
  2. គណនាអាំងតេក្រាលខាងក្រោម៖

    3. $\displaystyle\int (2x+3)^4\,dx$
    4. $\displaystyle\int 4(5x-2)^3\,dx$
    5. $\displaystyle\int \dfrac{1}{1+3x}\,dx$
    6. $\displaystyle\int \dfrac{1+e^x}{x+e^x}\,dx$
    7. $\displaystyle\int \dfrac{\ln x}{x}\,dx$
    8. $\displaystyle\int \dfrac{3x}{x^2+1}\,dx$
  3. គណនាអាំងតេក្រាលកំណត់ខាងក្រោម៖
